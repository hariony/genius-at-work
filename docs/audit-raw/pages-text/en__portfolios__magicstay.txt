##### Détails du projet
### MagicStay, the best accommodation for your business trips or professional mobility
The adventure began in 2013. At that time Valéry was working in an event management group which organized many international trade shows. He travelled a lot, alone and with his team, and was not allowed to book apartments because they were not referenced in the SBT (Self Booking Tool) used by his company. The apartments did not meet security requirements and could not be booked immediately. However, Valery used this type of accommodation during his holidays and appreciated the space, independence and feeling of being at home.
